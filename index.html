About Us

Our Story

Palette & Canvas was founded in 2018 by award-winning artist Elena Rodriguez, who envisioned a collaborative space where artists could work, learn, and exhibit together.

What began as a small workshop space has grown into a thriving community art center, with over 500 members ranging from beginners to professional artists.

Our mission is to make art accessible to everyone by providing affordable studio access, quality instruction, and exhibition opportunities in a supportive environment.

We believe that artistic expression enriches lives and strengthens communities, and we're dedicated to fostering creativity in people of all ages and backgrounds.
